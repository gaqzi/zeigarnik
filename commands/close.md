description: Close this session's goal with zg, then write the handoff
argument-hint: [how it went]
---

The human is closing this zg session. $ARGUMENTS

1. Ask whether the goal is achieved, failed (and why) or cancelled, unless
   they just said. Record it with mcp__zg__close_goal and their words.
2. For each open action, ask whether it is done, cancelled, or to be filed;
   record it with mcp__zg__action (export lists it in the handoff).
3. Propose the handoff: the next session's one goal, the decisions it must
   respect (lasting or not), and the few references it needs. Once they
   agree, write it with mcp__zg__handoff and show what it returns verbatim.
